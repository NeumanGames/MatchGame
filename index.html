How this works & where the data comes from

Sunrise, sunset, and civil twilight are calculated for your exact coordinates and date using standard solar-position equations (the same approach used by NOAA's solar calculator) — accurate to about a minute anywhere in the world.

Weather forecasts come from Open-Meteo and are only available roughly 16 days out. Beyond that, the Month Trend view shows daylight patterns only — weather that far ahead can't be reliably predicted.

Civil twilight is the window right before sunrise / after sunset when there's still usable light but the sun is below the horizon — this is when reflective gear matters most, since drivers' eyes are adjusting.

This planner doesn't store your location or trip details anywhere — everything runs in your browser and resets on reload.

Note: location and weather lookups need a live internet connection to reach outside map/weather services. They won't work if this file is opened directly from disk in some browsers, or inside a network-restricted preview — host it on a real web address (GitHub Pages works well) for full functionality.
