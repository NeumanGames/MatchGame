❓ How to Play
  • 🃏Flip two cards — tap any card to flip it over and see what's hiding!
  • 👀Find the match — if both cards show the same thing, they're a pair!
  • 🎉Win a point — matched pairs pop off the board and you keep your turn!
  • 🔄Miss? — the cards flip back and the next player takes a turn.
  • 🏆Most matches wins — keep going until all the cards are gone!
