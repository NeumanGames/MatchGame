❓ Game Options
  • 🔢Numbers — count emoji icons to find matching pairs (e.g. five 🌈 = five 🌈).
  • 🔤Letters — match capital letters with their animal or object (e.g. H · 🐴 · Horse).
  • 🎨Colors — match color-coded cards by name and emoji.
  • ⭐Shapes — match shape emoji cards by name.
  • 🌟Levels — start with 🌱 Seedling (6 cards) and work up to 💥 EXTREME! (48 cards).
  • 📐Card size — choose Small, Medium, or Large before starting. Change anytime with S · M · L in the toolbar.
  • 👥Players — 1 to 4 players take turns. Match a pair to keep your turn and earn a point!

❓ How to Play
  • 🃏Flip two cards — tap any card to reveal what's hiding!
  • 👀Find the match — if both cards show the same thing, it's a pair!
  • 🎉Score a point — matched pairs pop off the board and you keep your turn.
  • 🔄Miss? — cards flip back and the next player takes a turn.
  • 🏆Most matches wins — keep going until all cards are gone!
  • 📐Resize cards — tap S · M · L in the toolbar anytime.
  • 🏠Home button — pause and return to the menu or start a new game.
